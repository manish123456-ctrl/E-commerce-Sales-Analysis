# 🛒 Ecommerce Sales Analysis – BlinkIT Grocery Data

This project focuses on analyzing **E-commerce sales data** of BlinkIT grocery stores using **Microsoft Excel** and **Power BI**.  
The goal of this analysis is to uncover sales trends, outlet performance, and customer preferences to provide actionable business insights.

---

## 📊 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, formatting, and initial exploration  
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and visualization  

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales**
- **Average Sales**
- **Number of Items**
- **Average Rating**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights
The Power BI dashboard provides a detailed overview of sales performance through various visualizations:

- **Total Sales by Fat Content**
- **Total Sales by Item Type**
- **Fat Content by Outlet (for Total Sales)**
- **Total Sales by Outlet Establishment Year**
- **Total Sales by Outlet Size**
- **Total Sales by Outlet Location**
- **All Metrics by Outlet**

---

## 💡 Key Findings
- **Tier 3 outlets** recorded the highest total sales compared to other tiers.  
- **Regular Fat** items performed better in sales than **Low Fat** items.  
- **Supermarket Type 1** contributed the most to total sales.  
- Larger outlets tended to have higher sales averages.
